1. 分束器矩阵的定义

题目给出的分束器的矩阵形式是
[rititr]\begin{bmatrix} r & it \\ it & r \end{bmatrix}[rit​itr​]
其中 rrr 和 ttt 分别代表反射和透射的振幅。
The beam splitter is defined by the matrix
[rititr]\begin{bmatrix} r & it \\ it & r \end{bmatrix}[rit​itr​]
where rrr and ttt denote the reflection and transmission amplitudes, respectively.

2. 电路结构与输入输出

3. 后选（Postselection）

输出态需要经过后选，筛选出特定的探测结果。这样可以剔除不希望出现的输出，只保留符合CNOT门逻辑的情况。
The output state requires postselection, selecting only certain detector outcomes. This removes unwanted outputs and leaves only those that match the logic of the CNOT gate.

1. 问题背景与物理意义

这是一个典型的玻色子采样（boson sampling, 玻色子采样）问题。光子通过量子线路的酉矩阵进行干涉（interference），输出分布概率由酉矩阵的子矩阵的Permanent决定。

This is a typical boson sampling problem. Photons interfere through the unitary matrix of the quantum circuit, and the output probability distribution is determined by the permanent of the submatrices of the unitary.
